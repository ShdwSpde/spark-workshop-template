## Description (paste into Luma)

Most mission-driven organizations run on too few people doing the work of too many. Grant deadlines, the newsletter, the social posts, the funder research. It piles up.

Spark is a multi-agent AI system that runs an organization's operations. Radical Imagination built it to run a studio of two dozen projects with one founder. This is a live look at what it can actually do.

We know our full build-it-yourself workshop is not for everyone, and that is the point of this morning. No laptops, no setup, no technical background needed. Come watch, ask hard questions, drink good coffee.

We will put two agents to work in real time:

- The Funding Agent: finds grants your org qualifies for, drafts the letter of inquiry, builds a funder strategy.
- The Social Content Agent: writes your newsletter, your posts, your content calendar, in your voice.

We are demoing these two because every organization, whatever its mission, needs money and needs an audience.

Bring the operations problem that is eating your week. We will point an agent at it, and you can see for yourself.

Free coffee, on us.
